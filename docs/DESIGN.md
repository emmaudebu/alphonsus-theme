## Brand & Style
The design system articulates an educational sanctuary that marries the wonder of children's literature with the precision of language acquisition (specifically Swedish learning programs). It moves away from generic, unconsidered WooCommerce storefront aesthetics toward an inviting, literary editorial experience.

The aesthetic blends **Warm Editorial** with **Modern Tactile Commerce**. It honors physical books through paper-warm canvas backgrounds, thoughtful typographic hierarchies, crisp slate ink tones, and tangible book-card elevations. The voice is authoritative yet tender, scholarly yet accessible to parents, educators, and curious young readers alike.

Key brand attributes:
- **Scholarly & Trustworthy**: Instills confidence through structured layouts, rigorous typography, and royal sapphire blue grounding.
- **Warm & Inspiring**: Radiates optimism through amber gold accents, parchment backdrops, and generous breathing room.
- **Thoughtfully Tactile**: Soft dimensional shadows evoke the touch of linen book covers, embossed library stamps, and cream book paper.

## Colors
The color palette deliberately departs from sterile, stark #FFFFFF backgrounds by implementing a soothing paper spectrum. Canvas surfaces evoke premium uncoated book stocks (`#FAFAF8` and `#F4F3EE`), establishing an unhurried, comfortable reading atmosphere.

### Functional Roles
- **Primary (`#2B3A8F` - Royal Sapphire Blue)**: The core foundational anchor. Used for brand marks, primary CTA buttons, major section headers, category tabs, and active navigational signals.
- **Secondary (`#E58D28` - Warm Amber Gold)**: The energetic educational spark. Reserved for high-value conversions, promotional badges, highlight ribbons, rating stars, and subtle accent underlines.
- **Tertiary (`#3E52A3` - Medium Indigo)**: Supporting depth for hover states on primary components, subtle borders on active states, and secondary visual details.
- **Neutral (`#1E2238` - Deep Slate Ink)**: Replaces harsh pure black with rich, high-contrast printing ink. Used for body text, book titles, price tags, and microcopy to guarantee effortless readability.
- **Specialized Surfaces**:
  - `canvas-paper` (`#FAFAF8`): Main application page background.
  - `surface-cream` (`#F4F3EE`): Section breakouts, filter panels, and banner backgrounds.
  - `surface-card` (`#FFFFFF`): Elevated book jackets, catalog product cards, and modal containers.
  - `border-subtle` (`#E8E6DF`): Soft, unobtrusive hairline dividers that respect editorial white space.

## Typography
The system uses an intentional dual-type pairing that contrasts the classic dignity of bookbinding with contemporary digital utility:

- **Noto Serif (Headlines & Editorial Titles)**: Introduces warmth, literary credentials, and graceful rhythm. Used exclusively for section headlines, collection banners, author names, and product titles.
- **Plus Jakarta Sans (Interface, Body & Navigation)**: Offers soft terminals, exceptional clarity at micro sizes, and approachable geometric curves. Used for all navigational elements, filtering systems, price tags, button labels, and body descriptions.

Titles should never be forced into all-caps unless configured as small categorization tags (`label-sm`).

## Layout & Spacing
The layout follows a 12-column responsive fluid grid bounded inside a `1280px` maximum viewport container.

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with `24px` (`1.5rem`) gutters and `48px` page margins. E-commerce catalogs display 4 items per row; collection spotlights use an asymmetrical 5/7 split for feature books vs. curated bundles.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with `16px` gutters and `32px` page margins. Product grids shift to 3 columns; sidebars collapse into responsive horizontal filter carousels or bottom sheets.
- **Mobile (<768px)**: 4 columns with `16px` gutters and `16px` margins. Book cards display either in a structured 2-column grid with compact book jackets or in a fluid horizontal swipe carousel.

Generous vertical padding (`space-2xl` to `space-3xl`) separates bookstore collections (e.g., Story Books, Swedish School, Merch) to prevent visual overcrowding and allow each educational vertical to stand on its own merits.

## Elevation & Depth
Elevation mimics the physical depth of laid paper and book jackets resting gently on a library desk. Standard heavy drop-shadows are strictly avoided in favor of warm, low-opacity ambient occlusion.

- **Level 0 (Flat Surface)**: Clean canvas layer (`#FAFAF8` or `#F4F3EE`) with 1px border (`#E8E6DF`) when separation is needed without lift.
- **Level 1 (Resting Book Card / Sheet)**: `box-shadow: 0 4px 16px -2px rgba(30, 34, 56, 0.05), 0 1px 3px 0 rgba(30, 34, 56, 0.03);` on pure white background, framed with a subtle border `#E8E6DF`.
- **Level 2 (Card Hover & Quick View Peek)**: `box-shadow: 0 12px 28px -6px rgba(43, 58, 143, 0.10), 0 4px 10px -2px rgba(30, 34, 56, 0.04);` with a `-4px` vertical transform translation to signal tactile pickup.
- **Level 3 (Sticky Nav, Floating Cart & Modals)**: `box-shadow: 0 20px 40px -8px rgba(30, 34, 56, 0.12), 0 8px 16px -4px rgba(30, 34, 56, 0.04);` with a subtle backdrop filter (`backdrop-filter: blur(12px)` and 92% background opacity) to maintain contextual orientation.

## Shapes
The system implements roundedness level `2` (`rounded-lg: 1rem` / `rounded-xl: 1.5rem`), conveying approachability suitable for children's learning without losing institutional refinement.

- **Cards & Visual Containers**: Apply `1.25rem` to `1.5rem` (rounded-xl) with inner book covers clipped cleanly at `0.75rem`.
- **Form Controls & Inputs**: Set at `0.75rem` (rounded-md to rounded-lg) for comfortable thumb tapping.
- **Interactive Badges & Cart Pills**: Use full circular pill radii (`9999px`) for age-group pills, language level badges (A1, B1), and stock chips.

## Components

### 1. Buttons
- **Primary Action (Sapphire CTA)**: Solid `#2B3A8F` background with `#FFFFFF` label, `0.75rem` corner radius, `14px 28px` padding. Transitions on hover to `#3E52A3` with subtle vertical lift (`translateY(-1px)`).
- **Secondary Action (Amber Accent)**: Solid `#E58D28` background with `#FFFFFF` text. Used exclusively for primary purchase triggers like "Add to Cart" or "Enroll in Swedish School". Hover shifts to `#D67F1D`.
- **Ghost / Editorial Outline**: 1.5px border in `#2B3A8F`, transparent fill, text `#2B3A8F`. Hover fills with `#EEF1FA`.

### 2. Book Cards
- **Architecture**: Elevated Level 1 white container (`rounded-xl`) with `16px` inner padding.
- **Artwork Frame**: Book cover rendered with an intentional 3:4 or 1:1 book spine ratio, soft drop-shadow (`0 8px 16px rgba(0,0,0,0.08)`), and inset highlight to simulate real paper edges.
- **Meta Hierarchy**:
  1. Age/Level Chip (e.g., "Ages 4-8" or "Swedish A1").
  2. Book Title in `title-card` (`Noto Serif`, `#1E2238`).
  3. Star rating summary (amber `#E58D28` filled stars, slate review count).
  4. Price tag in `price-tag` (`#2B3A8F`).
  5. Quick "Add" icon button appearing smoothly on hover.

### 3. Category & Age Chips
- Unselected: Crisp paper background `#FFFFFF`, subtle border `#E8E6DF`, text `#60647A`.
- Selected: Solid `#2B3A8F` background with `#FFFFFF` text, paired with an amber indicator dot.

### 4. Search & Inputs
- Large search bar resting in the header: Height `48px`, background `#FFFFFF`, border `1px solid #E8E6DF`, text `#1E2238`. Focus state introduces a 2px outer ring of `#2B3A8F` at 20% opacity and tightens border color to `#2B3A8F`.

### 5. Educational Feature Banners (Swedish School / Bundles)
- Asymmetrical split banner with gentle two-tone background (`#EEF1FA` to `#F4F3EE`), large editorial display headline, list of curricular highlights with amber checkmarks, and clear dual CTAs.
